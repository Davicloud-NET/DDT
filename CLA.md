Thank you for wanting to contribute to Davicloud's open-source software. This agreement says
which rights you give Davicloud in what you contribute, and what Davicloud may and may not do with
them. You accept it once, for all of Davicloud's open-source projects that use it.

In short, and this summary is not part of the agreement:

- You keep your copyright, and you stay free to use your contribution in any way you like.
- Your contribution is published under the licence of the project you contribute to, like the
  rest of that project.
- Davicloud may license it under other free licences only within fixed limits. What you contribute
  to a project under a GNU licence stays under the copyleft licences of the GNU family.
- You confirm that the contribution is yours to give.

## 1. Who agrees with whom

This agreement is between:

To accept you must be at least 18 years old, or have the consent of your legal guardian.

## 2. What the words mean

- A **Project** is a software project that Davicloud publishes under a free and open-source
  licence and whose repository says that it takes contributions under this agreement, with
  everything that belongs to it, such as its documentation, tests and build scripts. DDT, the
  Davicloud Deployment Toolkit, is one.
- A **Contribution** is any work that you submit to Davicloud so that it becomes part of a
  Project: code, documentation, translations, tests, images, designs and anything else, new or as
  a change to what exists. It makes no difference whether you submit it as a pull request, as a
  patch, or as text or a file in an issue or a discussion. Something you clearly mark as not a
  contribution is not one.
- **The Project's licence** is the licence under which Davicloud publishes the Project, together
  with any additional terms and additional permissions, as the Project's repository states them
  when you submit the Contribution.
- A **GNU licence** is the GNU General Public License, the GNU Lesser General Public License or
  the GNU Affero General Public License, in any version.

## 3. What this agreement is for

This agreement covers only Projects, which are free and open-source software when you contribute
to them. Davicloud also makes other software, which it does not cover. Davicloud looks after each
Project as a whole: it develops, maintains and publishes it, and decides under which of the
licences that section 6 allows it is published. This agreement gives Davicloud the rights it needs
for that in every Contribution, now and later, without having to ask each contributor again.
Section 6 keeps your Contribution under free licences, whatever becomes of the Project later.

You keep your copyright in your Contribution and every right this agreement does not grant. You
may go on using, publishing and licensing your Contribution yourself, on any terms you choose.

5. to translate, adapt, rearrange and otherwise change it, to combine it with other works, and to

6. to grant others licences in it, within the limits of section 6.

The licence also covers ways of using a work that are not known today, as far as the law allows
you to grant that in the form in which you accept this agreement. Where the law gives you a right
to revoke the licence for such a new way of use, you keep that right. Davicloud may tell you of a
new way of use under section 13.

Davicloud may not license your Contribution to others on any other terms, unless you agree to
that separately. Whoever takes Davicloud's place under section 11 is bound by this limit too.

If you hold or control a patent, now or later, that using your Contribution would infringe, by

changing and passing on the Contribution and works that contain it.

Whoever brings a claim in court that a Project or a Contribution to it infringes a patent loses
this licence on the day the claim is filed.

You receive no payment for a Contribution or for the licences in this agreement.

Beyond section 9 you give no warranty for a Contribution. You provide it as it is, you need not
maintain it, and you are liable only for damage you cause on purpose or by gross negligence.

Davicloud need not include a Contribution in a Project, keep it there, or publish it.

Mandatory law stays untouched by this section.

## 11. Who may take Davicloud's place

It makes no difference to this agreement under which name or registered firm the person named in
section 1 runs Davicloud.

Davicloud may pass on its rights and duties under this agreement, for all Projects or for single
ones, to:

- a company or other legal person to which the Project is transferred or contributed, alone or
  together with Davicloud's business;
- anyone who later takes over the Project from it.

You agree to this now, so that it needs no further consent from you. Whoever takes Davicloud's
place is bound by this agreement as Davicloud is. Davicloud announces such a change in the
Project's repository.

## 12. Ending the agreement

You may end this agreement for the future at any time by writing to contact@davicloud.net. It
then no longer applies to what you submit afterwards, and Davicloud takes no further
Contributions from you until you accept it again.

The licences in every Contribution you submitted before that stay in force.

To be able to prove this agreement, Davicloud keeps the name and number of the GitHub account you
accept with, the time you accepted, and the version of the agreement. You may add a name and an
email address, and need not. The name can be your legal name or the name you are known by as a
contributor. Davicloud keeps all of this for as long as it relies on a licence you granted, does
not publish what you added, and uses an email address only for notices under this agreement. A
Project's version history, with the name and email address your commits carry, is public. The
privacy notice at [link] says more, including who processes the data for Davicloud and which
rights you have.

Davicloud sends notices under this agreement to the email address you gave, and you can change it
at any time. If you gave none, Davicloud sends them by mentioning your GitHub account in the
Project's repository.

## 14. Law, courts and the rest

Austrian law governs this agreement, without its rules that would refer to another country's law
and without the United Nations Convention on Contracts for the International Sale of Goods. If
you are a consumer, you keep the protection of the mandatory rules of the country you live in.

The courts with jurisdiction for [place], Austria, decide disputes arising from this agreement.
If you are a consumer, the courts that the law names for consumers stay open to you, and Davicloud
can bring a claim against you only there.

If a part of this agreement is invalid, the rest stays in force.

A new version of this agreement applies only to what you submit after you have accepted that
version. Earlier Contributions stay under the version you accepted for them.

## 15. How you accept

You accept this agreement on the signing page that the check on your pull request links to,
signed in with your GitHub account, by confirming that you agree. From then on it applies to
every Contribution you submit to any Project, including those in pull requests that were open at
that moment, until you end it under section 12.
